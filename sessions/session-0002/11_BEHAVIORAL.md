# Round 11 — Behavioral / Leadership

Use a real example; do not borrow ownership from a repository description.

Tell me about a time when a delivery commitment conflicted with a reliability or correctness risk.

Cover:

- situation and business stakes;
- your exact responsibility and decision authority;
- the evidence you gathered;
- options and trade-offs you communicated;
- how you influenced people who disagreed;
- the action, measurable result, and any remaining downside;
- what you learned and what you would now do differently.

Follow-up: if the deadline could not move, what minimum guardrail would you insist on and what would you explicitly defer?
