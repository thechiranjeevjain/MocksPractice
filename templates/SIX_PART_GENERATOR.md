# Six-Part Cold Reconstruction

Fill this before writing code or opening sources.

## TRIGGER

What wording, constraints, or failure mode should make this model come to mind?

## PATTERN

What reusable problem/design family applies?

## INVARIANT

What must remain true throughout the algorithm or design?

## TEMPLATE

Write the smallest reusable control-flow or architecture skeleton from memory.

## FALLBACK

What brute-force/simple approach works, and precisely why is it too slow or limited?

## OPTIMIZATION

What removes the bottleneck, and what trade-off does it introduce?
