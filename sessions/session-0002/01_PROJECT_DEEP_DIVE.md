# Round 1 — Project Deep Dive

Choose one project you genuinely implemented or studied deeply. Good source-backed anchors include pre-trade risk, market data, matching/order flow, or the notification platform. Do not claim production ownership you did not have.

From a blank page, cover:

1. The user/business problem and the exact boundary you owned.
2. The critical request/event path in no more than eight steps.
3. One correctness invariant and one latency/availability objective.
4. One failure you designed for and how the system detects and recovers from it.
5. One trade-off you made, what you rejected, and why.
6. Evidence: code, test, measurement, incident, or review feedback that substantiates the claim.
7. What you would change at 10x traffic and what you would deliberately keep simple.

Finish with a 90-second senior-level summary that clearly separates `I owned`, `I contributed`, and `I learned`.
